# Sunday research source watchlist — effective 2026-10-25

This is a **prospective operational research instruction**, not an amendment
to frozen forecasting Protocol v1.0 or a revision of historical assessments.

## Required Iran International coverage scan

Every Sunday **before the evidence cutoff and before any probability elicitation**,
check original Iran International reporting directly, separately from Reuters,
AP and official/primary-source searches:

- English latest news: https://www.iranintl.com/en/latest
- English publication: https://www.iranintl.com/en
- Persian publication: https://www.iranintl.com/ when accessible and
  adequately translatable; do not silently substitute a translation
  for a verified original quotation.
- Supplement direct-site inspection with date-bounded searches for original
  `iranintl.com` stories since the last Sunday assessment. Search for domestic
  protests, strikes, living costs and the rial; elite divisions and leadership;
  Revolutionary Guards, police, internal-security deployments and defections;
  shortages, energy, financial distress and payments; and military/escalation
  developments relevant to the frozen four scored events.

Record the **actual scan time**, search window, topics, whether access was
possible, and any materially relevant *original* story URLs in the evidence
collection working audit **before closing evidence**. Where no relevant
original report is found, document "searched; no eligible new evidence"
rather than inventing a report or treating absence of reporting as proof
of stability. If access/search fails, record the failure or coverage gap
explicitly; do not assert that the outlet was successfully checked.

## Eligibility and corroboration

Iran International is an additional **candidate source**, never an automatic
validation, mandatory citation, or additional independent confirmation merely
because it republishes a wire, official statement or another outlet.

For every possibly material claim:
1. Identify the original publisher, URL, author/byline, **event date** and
   **first public release date/time**, when independently recoverable. Mark
   missing or ambiguous publication times unknown; never backfill guessed times.
2. Verify the item was publicly available on or before the assessment's
   actual closed evidence cutoff. Exclude newer information from forecasts.
3. Check whether it describes firsthand reporting, an attributed statement,
   an opinion/analysis, a syndicated wire, or unverified/anonymous claims.
   A quote from an official does not itself corroborate the official's claim.
4. Seek independent original reporting, primary documents, or observable
   evidence for consequential claims (especially protests, casualties,
   security-force defections, elite fractures, currency levels and military
   actions). For uncorroborated claims, explicitly label the uncertainty,
   use an appropriate reliability classification, and do not present them
   as established facts or apply an unjustified quantitative revision.
5. Deduplicate with Reuters, AP and all earlier evidence IDs by the underlying
   reporting/event, not just distinct URLs; carried-forward evidence retains
   its historical identifier.

The source scan never obliges inclusion of an Iran International article
in the published evidence ledger. Use only relevant, attributable,
cutoff-eligible reporting; retain countervailing information and the
normal full validation plus alternating affirmative/critical review gates.

Do not rewrite earlier forecasts, source provenance, the published history,
or Protocol v1.0. Do not enable X announcements as part of this source change.
