# Sunday research source watchlist — effective 2026-10-25

## V9 cross-stage access audit: model narration versus HTTP evidence

Preserve three independent records for each original research URL: the
research model's original unverified description of search-stage page access,
the later Python metadata-only request's recorded status and publisher
metadata, and any separate exact passage retrieved for an individual claim.
Original search-stage text may describe a readable excerpt when the later
metadata GET fails; it may describe HTTP 403 when a later metadata request
succeeds. This does not itself prove a contradiction or that either stage
verified a full article.

Require review of actual source identity, chronology, independently
retrievable passage scope, and reported limitations. Do not transform model
comments about earlier access into a system HTTP log, and do not treat
publisher metadata as complete original article access or authenticated
first publication. A reviewer unable to reconcile a material access
discrepancy must state the canonical URL and mark the audit incomplete;
no issue is silently waived to achieve a clean report. No fabricated
observations, corroboration, accepted-evidence minimum or publication.


## V8: document historical context, page access and search timing precisely

For new frozen V8 source snapshots distinguish the model's **search-stage
candidate outcome** from the **after-screen accepted evidence**. A category
may locate material even if no admissible current observation survives.
Record both states, not an assumed event or an assumed absence.

A successfully fetched HTML page's publisher metadata is not evidence that
the full original article body was independently inspected. Distinguish
metadata-only access from an exact individually verified text-passage
witness. A publisher-reported original timestamp is not independently
verified first release, even if it contains a timezone offset. Old
protests, earlier sanctions, past humanitarian deliveries and historical
analyses stay historical background, not current fresh observations.
Later inspection does not shift the original research cutoff, and the
post-snapshot interval remains unsearched.

A complete, accurately described six-category **source audit** need not
justify a geopolitical inference. It may end as complete and inconclusive
only after affirmative AND critical reviewers independently agree the
audit is accurate and complete, and identify only the separate failure
to support a forecast. Unresolved conflicts in original-source access,
citation matching, reported chronology or coverage still block audit
completion and do not become harmless limitations.


## Prospective evidence quantity is not a quota

A search must examine six categories, but there is **no fixed minimum**
of accepted source observations, different publishers, new events, or
raises/lowers/ambiguous observed outcomes. Sources need genuine original
support, not inclusion to fill a table. When no admissible records exist,
return an empty evidence list and explain the gaps. Search effort,
factual provenance, the closed cutoff and both substantive reviews
remain mandatory. Reviewer disagreement or unsupported inferences block
clean approval; sparse evidence is not evidence of no risk.

## As-of time versus review-processing time

A completed six-category sweep covers only information eligible at the
original immutable snapshot as-of time. A later metadata check or delayed
review is NOT a fresh search through the intervening period. Source audits
must label the original as-of time, later processing clock and unsearched
post-snapshot interval separately; unknown first publication stays unknown.
This protects a prior-day resumed diagnostic from being misrepresented as
current-day coverage.

## Mandatory original-source quality audit, before forecast elicitation

Every DISTINCT original public-HTTPS URL selected in the six source
categories or as NEW evidence must receive one explicit assessment:
`kind` (direct observation, attributed assertion, analytical opinion,
unverified report, or background only), `origin` (original reporting,
syndicated/reprint, official/advocacy claim, secondary analysis, unknown),
specific assessment and limitations, and claimed corroborating original
URLs (empty when none can be independently established).

The source client attempts first-publication metadata extraction for
**every URL**, including think-tank/background references not scored as
new events. An inaccessible page, no timestamp, partial snippet, wire
mirror or think-tank estimate is identified as such; never transform it
into independently verified data merely because its URL was cited. The
`source-quality-audit.json` records each URL's full role, exposure and
timestamp limitations and the original editor's analytical classification.
Publisher metadata timestamps are *publisher-supplied*, not externally
authenticated release clocks. Keep historical carried-forward evidence
immutable; do not invent retrospective source-publication times.
A finding of an Houthi strike does not by itself demonstrate new Iranian
material or technical resupply.

The two alternating reviewers must assess **every URL separately**,
record a substantive finding and either accept it as explicitly qualified
support/background or reject its insufficient evidence. A missing row,
unsupported independent-corroboration claim, unqualified material inference
or a rejected source blocks a clean review or forecast publication. URL
integrity, model assessments and the existence of the audit are not
themselves proof of claim truth.

## Mandatory URL-level citation reconciliation

Every newly selected media, original research, and think-tank link must
have the exact original public HTTPS URL visible in the API's actual
search results and citation metadata. Source provenance is tracked by
both the six-category sweep and the new evidence ledger, without
accepting look-alike article IDs or unverified alternative publisher
URLs. Record unsupported claims **unchanged** with their URL and reason
in the private citation-reconciliation audit; do not silently remove,
rewrite, or reclassify them as verified. A flagged claim blocks the
closed source set, any new forecast or publication. A read-only
diagnostic may end with a flagged failure report, retaining all
questionable links for subsequent independent checking.

## Comprehensive media and think-tank discovery (controlling expansion)

**Scope:** Search broadly across *all relevant, accessible, credible original
media and policy/academic research*, not a fixed whitelist of a few wire
services. It is impossible to literally examine every reputable outlet in
the world each week, so use **adaptive, topic-specific discovery plus a
documented cross-category coverage sweep**, starting with the examples below
and adding credible emerging sources. Names are **starting points, not
blanket endorsements**, and no organization is automatically mandatory to
cite. Maintain the named Iran International scan below.

### Source categories to scan every Sunday

1. **Official/primary evidence and direct observations:** government
   ministries and central banks (including Iranian, Gulf, US and European
   institutions); the IAEA; IMF, World Bank, BIS, UN agencies and UNCTAD;
   IEA, EIA, OPEC, customs/trade and shipping authorities; primary maritime
   vessel movement or energy-production datasets; contemporaneous
   legislation, company filings, satellite imagery, and independently
   documented eyewitness materials. Record institutional incentives and
   measurement limitations; an official assertion is not automatically
   factual corroboration.
2. **International news and original wire reporting:** Reuters, AP and AFP;
   BBC, Financial Times, Bloomberg, Wall Street Journal, New York Times,
   Washington Post, The Guardian, The Economist, CNN, DW, France 24,
   NPR, Nikkei and additional professionally edited outlets discovered
   by topical searching. Separate original reporting from syndicated
   copies, rewrites and opinion pieces.
3. **Regional, Persian-language and affected-country media:** Iran
   International, BBC Persian, Radio Farda/RFE-RL, DW Persian, IranWire,
   Al Jazeera (English/Arabic), Al-Monitor, The National, Arab News,
   Asharq Al-Awsat, Anadolu, Haaretz and other independently attributable
   Gulf, Turkish, Israeli, Iraqi, Yemeni and Iranian reporting. Read
   original Persian/Arabic reporting where reliable interpretation is
   possible. Include Iranian state outlets such as IRNA/Fars/Tasnim as
   **attributed regime claims**, not independent confirmation of those claims.
4. **Specialist and investigative media/monitoring:** shipping, oil, gas
   and payments coverage from Lloyd's List, S&P Global Commodity
   Insights, Argus, verified maritime-data providers and specialist
   financial press; Bellingcat and credible open-source investigators;
   ACLED event data where methodology supports comparison; Amnesty
   International, Human Rights Watch and other careful rights monitors
   for verified repression/casualty reports. Explicitly distinguish
   measured observations, modeled estimates, rumors and advocacy.
5. **Security, diplomacy, conflict and regional think tanks:** IISS,
   Chatham House, International Crisis Group, RUSI, CSIS, Carnegie
   Endowment, Brookings, RAND, Stimson Center, Atlantic Council,
   Middle East Institute, Wilson Center, SIPRI, Arab Gulf States
   Institute in Washington, Gulf International Forum, Washington
   Institute for Near East Policy, Quincy Institute, Foundation for
   Defense of Democracies, and credible research institutions in
   Iran, Israel, Gulf states, Europe and Asia. Treat viewpoints,
   policy recommendations and scenario estimates as **analysis**,
   not automatically as new observed events. Identify authors,
   evidence base, affiliations and possible institutional bias.
6. **Economic, humanitarian, academic and countervailing research:**
   IMF, World Bank, IIF, UN agencies, central-bank/economic research,
   peer-reviewed scholarship, humanitarian field reports, energy
   studies and reputable regional economic institutes. Where evidence
   favors sharply different interpretations, deliberately seek
   well-supported **countervailing** assessments rather than sampling
   only high-escalation or regime-collapse narratives.

**Adaptive topical discovery:** Use broad queries plus source-specific and
cross-language searches since the prior Sunday. Follow credible first-hand
citations to original articles, newly publishing specialists and direct
data rather than restricting results to a preselected outlet list. For
each important topic (internal legitimacy/protests, elite/coercive cohesion,
currency/payment stress, sanctions/oil/China credit, Hormuz/shipping,
Houthis/regional military risk, inflation/humanitarian conditions), look
for both confirming and contradicting reports across several different
source categories when reporting exists.

**Audit the sweep BEFORE the evidence cutoff and forecast elicitation:**
Record actual scan time/window, topical searches, outlet classes inspected,
links retrieved, access restrictions, item classifications and underlying
source independence. For each class note at least one of "eligible original
evidence found", "searched; no eligible new evidence", or "inaccessible /
incomplete", with concrete support for the status; an empty result is not
evidence of stability. The pre-cutoff **working coverage audit is separate**
from the scored forecast objects and public evidence ledger. Never fabricate
a search, retrieval, publication timestamp, report, conflict resolution, or
the number of independent sources. Disclose meaningful coverage gaps.

**Source-quality rules:** Evaluate **each claim**, not just an outlet's
reputation. Check first publication time against cutoff, date of event,
original URL/byline, corroboration, independence, methods and conflicts.
Assign analytical confidence rather than mechanically giving every outlet
equal weight. Think-tank estimates and advocacy opinions are NOT
observations, and repeating a Reuters/AFP/AP wire, official claim or
think-tank citation never creates another independent confirmation.
Do not invent a numerical revision from a weakly supported claim.
An inability to cover the entire world's media must not be represented
as an exhaustive or successful universal scan; disclose the bounded,
risk-focused breadth achieved and any missing material classes.

explicitly; do not assert that the outlet was successfully checked.

## Eligibility and corroboration

Iran International is an additional **candidate source**, never an automatic
validation, mandatory citation, or additional independent confirmation merely
because it republishes a wire, official statement or another outlet.

For every possibly material claim:
1. Identify the original publisher, URL, author/byline, **event date** and
   **first public release date/time**, when independently recoverable. Mark
   missing or ambiguous publication times unknown; never backfill guessed times.
2. Verify the item was publicly available on or before the assessment's
   actual closed evidence cutoff. Exclude newer information from forecasts.
3. Check whether it describes firsthand reporting, an attributed statement,
   an opinion/analysis, a syndicated wire, or unverified/anonymous claims.
   A quote from an official does not itself corroborate the official's claim.
4. Seek independent original reporting, primary documents, or observable
   evidence for consequential claims (especially protests, casualties,
   security-force defections, elite fractures, currency levels and military
   actions). For uncorroborated claims, explicitly label the uncertainty,
   use an appropriate reliability classification, and do not present them
   as established facts or apply an unjustified quantitative revision.
5. Deduplicate with Reuters, AP and all earlier evidence IDs by the underlying
   reporting/event, not just distinct URLs; carried-forward evidence retains
   its historical identifier.

The source scan never obliges inclusion of an Iran International article
in the published evidence ledger. Use only relevant, attributable,
cutoff-eligible reporting; retain countervailing information and the
normal full validation plus alternating affirmative/critical review gates.

Do not rewrite earlier forecasts, source provenance, the published history,
or Protocol v1.0. Do not enable X announcements as part of this source change.
