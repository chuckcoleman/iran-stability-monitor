Purpose

This project tracks Iran's political stability, regional escalation, maritime trade, foreign-exchange stress, energy stress, external support, and Iran-linked Houthi capabilities using public information.

Evidence classes

Observation is a reported or measured fact attributed to a source. Inference is an interpretation of observations. Probability estimate is a forward-looking analytical judgment, not a published statistic or market-implied probability.

Revision policy

Each dated forecast is preserved. Material revisions are accompanied by a reason and source links. Factual corrections are identified rather than silently rewriting forecast history.

Definitions

FX crisis: disorderly parallel-market depreciation or a foreign-currency/payment shortage severe enough to require broad emergency controls or materially disrupt normal imports and payments.

Hanke hyperinflation: inflation exceeding 50% per month for at least 30 consecutive days. Monthly reports may also use exchange-rate-implied inflation as an early-warning signal.

Major restructuring: substantial redistribution of governing power within Islamic Republic institutions without ending that institutional system.

Regime-ending event: loss or replacement of the Islamic Republic's governing institutional order.

Source policy

Priority goes to primary documents, official data, reputable international reporting and clearly identified specialist sources. Claims from governments, belligerents or opposition groups are treated as claims unless corroborated.

Indicator system

The underlying assessment monitors monetary/FX, fiscal, banking/payment, food/medicine, energy/industrial, protest, coercive-apparatus, elite-cohesion and external-support indicators. Missing data are unknown, not benign.

Forecast evaluation

The timestamped archive is intended to permit later calibration and scoring without hindsight revision.
