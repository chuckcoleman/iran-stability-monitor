# Prospective alternating affirmative and critical ChatGPT reviews — effective 2026-10-25

## V9 access-stage reconciliation required for audit completion

A source review must not conflate (a) the model's earlier original
web-search description of whether text was readable, partly readable,
blocked or returned an internal error, (b) a later independently executed
Python **HTML metadata-only** request, and (c) a specific captured original
article-body passage. An earlier model-reported HTTP 403 and a later metadata
HTTP 200 are not automatically contradictory; conversely neither outcome
authenticates full text or makes the other request succeed.

V9 binds the unchanged model statement and limitations plus its SHA-256 to
each original URL, and preserves the independently recorded metadata probe
and claim witness separately. Both affirmative and critical reviewers must
document actual channel comparison, disclose all unresolved **canonical
source URLs**, and withhold audit certification if any real inconsistency,
source identity conflict, or misleading access representation remains.
Two independent audit-completeness verdicts are still needed; an accurate,
complete source audit with no adequate current observations may finish as
**inconclusive / not publishable**. The forecast and full public report gates
remain independent and mandatory. Earlier V8 closed rejections remain final
under their frozen original review prompts.


## V8 independent audit-completeness versus inference-adequacy decisions

The nonpublishing research diagnostic now asks two separate questions.
For **audit completeness**, each perspective independently identifies all
six original research categories, every canonical source URL and review,
what original article bodies were actually inspected versus only publisher
metadata captured, release-date uncertainty, omitted/held evidence,
historical context and the unsearched post-cutoff interval. It may declare
`complete_with_disclosed_limitations` only when these records are accurate
and it has no unresolved audit-integrity objection.

For **inferential adequacy**, reviewers assess whether the admitted original
observations support a substantive geopolitical conclusion. Neither the
number of observations nor the number of publishers is a quota. If both
independently certify the *source audit* complete but report a single
well-supported `insufficient_for_inference` issue, the diagnostic may
finish as **source audit complete; inference inconclusive; NOT PUBLISHABLE**.
Both original model global `approved` flags remain **false**. This
distinction never certifies a scored forecast or report-wide approval;
Sunday report/forecast publication still requires its separately governed
full validation and review. No prior V1–V7 source snapshot is retroactively
reclassified. Any unresolved metadata, source identity, event chronology
or coverage-integrity defect means the audit is incomplete, not
`complete_with_disclosed_limitations`.


## V7 evidence quantity policy for new assessments

No numeric minimum is imposed on accepted observations, newly found
observations, independent publishers, or observed instability directions.
A research sweep may legitimately find **zero** acceptable observations.
It must still inspect all six required search categories and disclose
accessibility, timing, original-source, corroboration and coverage limits.

Both affirmative and critical source reviewers must evaluate the *exact*
admitted claim IDs, quantify what was available for review, and explain
what can and cannot be inferred. They must reject unsupported substantive
conclusions regardless of how many sources appear. A few qualifying
sources can proceed; no raw count creates automatic approval.
All other prospective forecast validations and publication safeguards remain
unchanged. Previously closed V1–V6 source reviews retain their original
decisions and versioned criteria.

## Source-specific dual-perspective quality gate

Before constructing any NEW prospective forecast, audit **every original
source URL**, including context-only specialist/media/think-tank entries
outside the newly selected evidence rows. Preserve source classifications,
original vs wire/syndicated vs unknown origin, direct retrieval availability,
publisher-supplied original date/time or UNKNOWN, independent corroborating
URLs and precise limitations. The data belong to a private
`source-quality-audit.json`, not an edit to prior history.

Both affirmative THEN critical reviewers must issue a substantive
per-URL `source_reviews` record (identical input digest), with a
qualified-support, background-only or unverified/rejected disposition.
The global verdict cannot be approved if any material source is explicitly
unverified/rejected. Disclose who made the judgment: a model's
classification or tool-cited URL is NOT an independent validation of a
reported event. Model omitting/duplicating any URL review is a failure.
Retain both per-source reviewers' real decisions as audit artifacts;
never silently remove troublesome sources to force an approval.

## Report-wide alternating test and review protocol (controlling, effective 2026-10-25)

The two successive ChatGPT editorial perspectives must test **EVERY
substantive component** of every new Iran Monitor assessment, Sunday report,
special/event-driven report and nonpublishing test report. A favorable
overall forecast review does **not** waive review of warnings, sources,
diagnostics, tables, charts, methodology, report prose or public summaries.

**Full-report inventory:** Use the `scripts/report_section_review.py`
fifteen-section inventory. It explicitly assigns every existing assessment
record field to exactly one section, and enumerates its individual evidence
IDs, news links, four scored forecasts, signed revisions, nine warning
indicators, political/regional scenario rows, causal-chain/absence entries,
conditions, Houthi diagnostics, six oil-credit adjustments, Hanke inflation,
dashboard metrics/statuses, limits and thresholds, social copy and
methodology/correction metadata. All six source-sweep classes must be
examined. An added or removed field, skipped row or changed table value
requires a new validated inventory; no unreviewed new data silently passes.

**Alternate full rounds:** Complete every deterministic/schema/citation,
arithmetic and rendering check first; then conduct an AFFIRMATIVE review
that tests support, arithmetic, representation and balancing evidence, and
a CRITICAL review that actively tests counterexamples, independence,
misleading implications, missing evidence, numeric assumptions, graphical
errors and omissions. Both review *the same unchanged SHA-256 full draft*.
For **each of the fifteen sections and all its inventoried items**, retain
in the round receipt the exact items, an actual specific substantive
test note, both role verdicts and issues. Global approval is permitted
only if every section passed each perspective with an empty issues list.
A failure in ANY section invalidates the round. Fix only permissible
evidence-closed unscored content, then rerun the entire deterministic suite
and BOTH perspectives on the revised hash; locked forecasts may not change.
The six-cycle and 60-minute limits remain hard failure ceilings.

**Full report artifact:** Record the full section-level affirmative and
critical findings in the Markdown review report with a heading for each
required section, and include the SHA-256 of the final complete machine
section-review matrix. The receipt validator verifies that hash, every
section and every item against the exact reviewed history object, and
that all corresponding section verdicts agree with the global two-role
verdicts. A receipt provides integrity and accountability, **not proof**
of genuine independent model instances, actual source retrieval, or
subjective reviewer correctness. The source-coverage audit and underlying
search provenance still require critical inspection, not just schema checks.

**All other report formats:** For reports or deliverables that are not
the Sunday history JSON (e.g., ad hoc situation reports, nonpublishing
test reports, appended tables or special analyses), prepare the same
positive/negative, section-by-section review log and disclose which
deterministic and retrieval checks actually ran. Call a test report PASS
only for the subsystems actually tested; if end-to-end elicitation,
publication, or an original source check did not run, mark these NOT
TESTED, not PASS. An ad hoc report without a native JSON receipt still
requires a transparent written audit of both perspectives.

**Derived public outputs:** Before deployment, ensure charts, tables and
dashboard text reproduce the exact reviewed validated data, with dates,
units and legends; visually inspect changes to actual renderings when
necessary. For each public summary/X post/thread made after issuance,
conduct a separate affirmative-then-critical review on its FINAL exact
text, probabilities, deltas, source support, character limits, status,
canonical URLs and deduplication. An unreviewed public message is not
authorized. If the connector cannot construct the permitted two-post
thread or verify it, omit posting; this does NOT retroactively invalidate
a separately validated assessment. An unmodified previously published
document is never retroactively rewritten.

Do NOT claim the mere existence of this policy or a passing JSON schema
proves substantive review occurred. Fail closed for missing report review
rows, unverifiable artifacts, mismatched hashes, known substantive
errors, timeout or incomplete tests.

The owner requires **affirmative THEN critical** review of the **identical SHA-256 draft**, repeating complete validation and both roles after any correction until BOTH record approved=true and issues=[] for the same draft. Six whole cycles and 60 minutes remain failure ceilings; neither an exhausted limit nor a warning/timeout equals approval. The closed evidence, four scored forecasts, dates, and previously issued records cannot be rewritten.

**Review provenance:** These are two sequential editorial perspectives within ChatGPT, **not two independent model instances**. The report and receipt must disclose this exactly; never imply independent execution, reuse approvals after a modification, or create an affirmative verdict that was not actually performed.

Version-3 receipts (from 2026-10-25 onward) identify origin chatgpt_alternating, review_mode affirmative_then_critical_chat, and reviewer_provenance one_chatgpt_session_two_perspectives. Include the immutable reviewed draft and full report, their SHA-256 digests, the actual time of each complete pre-review validation, affirmative and critical decisions on the same hash, every correction round's issues and new hash, final post-review validation time, review_completed_at matching the final critical decision, actual later Sunday issuance, and the unchanged four forecast locks. An earlier round may be superseded only when at least one role raised actionable issues, the revised draft SHA differs and the entire suite and BOTH roles reran. No post-review change except the actual issuance timestamp is permitted. Protected GitHub PR, exact-head CI, Pages artifact-byte and canonical-domain verification, and conditional deduplicated X posting remain mandatory.

**Historical compatibility and explicit version cutoff:** Only assessment dates **before October 18, 2026** may use version-1 `off_github` receipts. The issued **October 18, 2026** assessment uses its original version-2 `chatgpt_critical` receipt. Every assessment dated **October 25, 2026 or later** must use version-3 `chatgpt_alternating` receipts, including the exact reviewed draft/report and every full-section affirmative/critical decision. Version-1 approval with both legacy reviewers, even with internally consistent hashes, cannot authorize a new assessment after October 11. The archived `scripts/pre_github_review.py` tool produces version-1 receipts only and now refuses to prepare a future-era submission **before any expensive model call**; the current ChatGPT-first v3 process is mandatory. These are receipt-policy changes, not changes to frozen Protocol v1.0 or historical forecasts. A missing, ambiguous, untimestamped or failed review stops publication.

## Archived publication review policy, October 18, 2026 (historical only)

# Prospective single critical ChatGPT review — effective 2026-10-18

This operational policy changes the publication review requirement, NOT frozen forecasting Protocol v1.0. One documented critical review in ChatGPT replaces the former two-role AI review requirement. Legacy version-1 dual-review receipts remain valid.

The only permissible sequence is: verify immutable main baseline and current deployed artifact; close timestamped evidence; elicit and lock exactly four core forecasts once; run every deterministic check; conduct one documented rigorous critical review of the exact candidate with explicit approved=true and issues=[]; correct only permissible unscored narrative or diagnostics and restart all checks/review after changes; set the real Sunday issuance timestamp after approval; rerun every deterministic validator; prepare the matching version-2 receipt; submit a protected PR; verify exact-head CI and unchanged main; merge without bypass; verify Pages artifact bytes and canonical public JSON/HTML; consider X only if enabled, confirmed and not duplicative.

Version-2 review receipts must identify origin chatgpt_critical and mode single_critical_chat and include actual completion time; the report's SHA-256 and immutable reviewed-draft hash; baseline, issued candidate and prior-record digests; and the identical four locked forecasts. Keep both the full review report and the exact pre-issuance reviewed candidate in dated repository files. Only the top-level last record's publication_time may differ between reviewed and issued drafts; historical records, root metadata and all other current-record fields are immutable.

A ChatGPT review is an editorial decision, NOT proof of another independently executed model. The saved receipt authenticates contents only, not model identity. Never invent an affirmative review. All missing validations, review objections, action-requiring warnings, tool or timeouts and exhausted six-cycle/60-minute limits are failures, never approvals.

The off-GitHub dual-review CLI is an optional legacy alternative; its separate API-key requirement does not apply to the native ChatGPT review report workflow. Prior records, forecasting definitions and Protocol v1.0 remain frozen.
